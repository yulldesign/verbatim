This theme is a split. Setup took three weeks at one company and one afternoon at the other, and both participants explain the difference by technical capacity on the customer side, not by anything Pulse did.

At Lumen Logistics, an operations-led company of about 120 people, connecting the TMS took three weeks, partly because nobody on the team was technical and they had to wait for support callbacks [[source-interview-claire-martin]] [[connecting-the-tms-took-three-weeks-during-onboarding]] [[non-technical-teams-depend-on-support-callbacks-to-complete]]:

> "It was slow. It took us three weeks to connect our TMS."

> "Nobody on our side is technical, so we had to wait for support to call us back."

At Fretto, a startup of about forty people with developers, the integration was done in an afternoon without help [[source-interview-karim-benali]] [[teams-with-in-house-developers-set-up-the-integration-in-one]]:

> "Very fast for us, in one afternoon. We have devs, we connected the integration ourselves."

Karim anticipates Claire's experience without having lived it: « I understand it would be harder for a company without a technical team. » [[setup-is-expected-to-be-harder-for-companies-without-a]]. Once setup is done the friction seems to disappear: Claire finds the dashboards really clear afterwards [[dashboards-are-perceived-as-clear-once-setup-is-done]].

If this split holds, onboarding effort for non-technical customers is set by support availability, and those customers wait weeks before seeing value. Confidence is low: there is exactly one case on each side, and company size and integration type (TMS vs Karim's unspecified integration) vary along with technical capacity, so these factors cannot be separated.

## Evidence

- [[source-interview-claire-martin]] - three weeks to connect the TMS; no technical staff, waited on support callbacks.
- [[source-interview-karim-benali]] - one afternoon, connected by in-house developers; expects it to be harder for teams without technical staff.

## Open questions

- Does time-to-connect correlate with technical staffing across more onboardings, or with the type of system being connected (TMS vs other sources)?
- How much of Lumen's three weeks was waiting for support rather than the work itself?
- Would guided or done-for-you setup shorten onboarding for operations-led customers?

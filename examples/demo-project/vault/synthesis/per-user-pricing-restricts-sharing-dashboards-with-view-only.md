Both participants want to share Pulse dashboards with people who only need to look at them, and both say per-user billing stops them. The audience differs: an internal executive at Lumen Logistics, external customers at Fretto. The mechanism is the same. Every viewer costs a full seat, so access is rationed and sharing falls back on workarounds.

Claire Martin objects to the pricing model, not the price level [[source-interview-claire-martin]] [[per-user-pricing-charges-for-people-who-only-view]]:

> "The price is fine. What bothers me is paying per user when half the people only look."

Her CFO does not want a license, so she emails him screenshots instead, a workaround she calls makeshift [[stakeholders-without-an-account-can-only-be-shared]]. She adds that ten more viewers « would get hard to justify » [[per-user-pricing-charges-for-people-who-only-view]], which caps account growth, although she did not mention leaving.

Karim Benali shares dashboards with Fretto's customers through guest accounts, and the billing forced the company to cap access [[source-interview-karim-benali]] [[guest-accounts-billed-as-paying-users-limit-sharing-with]]:

> "We create guest accounts for them, but each guest counts as a paying user. We had to limit the number of customers who have access."

In his view this restricts the very thing his customers value most [[dashboard-access-is-what-customers-value-most]]: « It's a shame, that's exactly what they like the most. » The impact is sharper at Fretto, where dashboard sharing is part of the service to customers, than at Lumen, where the cost is one senior reader working from screenshots.

This matters because view-only users are where usage could spread (executives, customers), and the current model blocks that spread. Confidence is medium: both participants raise the issue consistently and concretely, but the sample is two.

## Evidence

- [[source-interview-claire-martin]] - price acceptable, but paying per user for view-only colleagues bothers her; CFO gets emailed screenshots; ten more viewers would be hard to justify.
- [[source-interview-karim-benali]] - guest accounts for customers count as paying users, so Fretto had to limit customer access to the dashboards customers like most.

## Open questions

- What share of seats across customers are view-only, and how many accounts cap viewers because of price?
- Would a free or cheap viewer tier, or public read-only links, change expansion, or would admins still restrict access for other reasons (security, data exposure to customers)?
- Is external (customer-facing) sharing a separate use case that needs its own packaging?

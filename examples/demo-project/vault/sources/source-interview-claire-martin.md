## Summary

Claire Martin heads operations at Lumen Logistics and manages a team of six tracking deliveries and delays. Interviewed on 2026-10-14 about the Pulse reporting tool, she describes rebuilding the weekly management report in Excel because Pulse figures diverged from the carrier's after an incident she could not explain to her director. Onboarding was slow (three weeks to connect the TMS), yet the live delivery map is used daily. She also raises sharing with non-account holders and per-user pricing for viewers.

## Themes

- Trust in data and the need for provenance
- Manual reporting workaround in Excel
- Slow integration-led onboarding
- Live delivery map as core daily value
- Sharing and per-user pricing for view-only stakeholders

## Participant profile

Operations manager accountable to management for delivery metrics, satisfied with the dashboards but wary of the numbers since a public error.

## Key quote

> "The director asked me where the gap came from and I had no answer. Since then, I don't trust the numbers until I've cross-checked them myself."

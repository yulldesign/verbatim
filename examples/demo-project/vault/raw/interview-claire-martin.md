User interview - "Pulse" reporting tool (fictional example)
Date: October 14, 2026 - Duration: 35 min
Interviewer: UX researcher

Interviewer: Thanks for making the time. Could you briefly introduce yourself?

Claire: Sure. I'm Claire Martin, head of operations at Lumen Logistics, we're about 120 people. I manage a team of six who track deliveries and delays.

Interviewer: How do you use Pulse today?

Claire: Every Monday morning I have to put together the weekly report for management. In theory Pulse does that on its own. In practice, I export the data to CSV and redo everything in Excel. It takes me a good two hours every Monday.

Interviewer: Why do you redo everything in Excel?

Claire: Because the numbers in Pulse don't always match the carrier's. Last time, I presented a 4% delay rate when it was actually 7%. The director asked me where the gap came from and I had no answer. Since then, I don't trust the numbers until I've cross-checked them myself.

Interviewer: What would help you?

Claire: Honestly, seeing where each number comes from. Just a link to the source, or the date of the last sync. If I know the data is from last night, I can explain it.

Interviewer: And getting started with the tool, at the beginning?

Claire: It was slow. It took us three weeks to connect our TMS. Nobody on our side is technical, so we had to wait for support to call us back. But once it was set up, the dashboards are really clear, my team loves the live delivery map.

Interviewer: You mentioned the map view, is that something you use often?

Claire: Every day. It's the first thing we open in the morning. It replaces calling the drivers to find out where they are.

Interviewer: Are there things you'd like to do that the tool doesn't allow?

Claire: Share a report with someone who doesn't have an account. Our CFO doesn't want a license, so I email him screenshots. It's a bit makeshift.

Interviewer: And what about pricing?

Claire: The price is fine. What bothers me is paying per user when half the people only look. If we had to add ten viewers, it would get hard to justify.

Interviewer: Thank you Claire, that's very clear.

Pulse is used for visualisation, but whenever its data has to be combined with internal data, everything is exported to CSV and moved into the company's data warehouse. The participant describes this as something he does "a lot" and backs it with a concrete pipeline (BigQuery). This shows Pulse is not the analysis endpoint for data teams; the warehouse is.

> "Pulse is good for visualizing, but as soon as we want to combine it with our internal data, I export everything to CSV and move it into our data warehouse."
> - Karim Benali, [[interview-karim-benali]]

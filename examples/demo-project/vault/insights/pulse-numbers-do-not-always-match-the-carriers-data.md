The participant states that Pulse figures diverge from the carrier's figures, with a concrete example: a delay rate shown as 4% that was actually 7%. A discrepancy of this size on a headline metric undermines the tool's value as a reporting source.

> "Because the numbers in Pulse don't always match the carrier's. Last time, I presented a 4% delay rate when it was actually 7%."
> - Claire Martin, [[interview-claire-martin]]

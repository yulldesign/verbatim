Pulse is meant to produce the weekly report for management, but the participant exports the data to CSV and rebuilds the whole report in Excel, which she says takes about two hours every Monday. This is a recurring, costly workaround that bypasses the product's core reporting job.

> "In theory Pulse does that on its own. In practice, I export the data to CSV and redo everything in Excel. It takes me a good two hours every Monday."
> - Claire Martin, [[interview-claire-martin]]

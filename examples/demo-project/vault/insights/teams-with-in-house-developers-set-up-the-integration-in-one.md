Setup at Fretto was very fast, taking a single afternoon, because the company has developers who connected the integration themselves. This shows that onboarding is smooth for technically equipped teams; the speed is attributed to their own capacity rather than to the product.

> "Very fast for us, in one afternoon. We have devs, we connected the integration ourselves."
> - Karim Benali, [[interview-karim-benali]]

Fretto shares dashboards with its customers through guest accounts, but each guest is billed as a paying user, so the company had to cap how many customers get access. The pricing model directly restricts the use case the participant identifies as most valued.

> "We create guest accounts for them, but each guest counts as a paying user. We had to limit the number of customers who have access."
> - Karim Benali, [[interview-karim-benali]]

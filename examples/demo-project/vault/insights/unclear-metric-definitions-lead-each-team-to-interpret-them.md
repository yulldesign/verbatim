Metric definitions are not documented anywhere, so teams interpret them differently. The participant gives a concrete example: whether a *delay* is measured against the promised slot or the estimated time. Inconsistent interpretations can lead teams to different conclusions from the same dashboard.

> "But the definitions aren't clear. A "delay", is that relative to the promised slot or to the estimated time? It's not written anywhere. So each team interprets it its own way."
> - Karim Benali, [[interview-karim-benali]]

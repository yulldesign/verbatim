With no technical staff on the customer side, setup stalled until support called back. Stated once; it suggests (inference) that self-serve integration is not realistic for operations-led customers and that support availability sets the onboarding pace.

> "Nobody on our side is technical, so we had to wait for support to call us back."
> - Claire Martin, [[interview-claire-martin]]

The participant finds the price itself acceptable; her objection is to the per-user model, since about half of the users only consult the data. Stated once and clearly, in a single answer: the issue is the pricing structure, not the level.

> "The price is fine. What bothers me is paying per user when half the people only look."
> - Claire Martin, [[interview-claire-martin]]

She goes on to say that more view-only seats would be difficult to defend:

> "If we had to add ten viewers, it would get hard to justify."
> - Claire Martin, [[interview-claire-martin]]

Inference (low confidence): the per-user model caps account expansion to view-only stakeholders and could become a renewal or growth risk; she did not mention leaving the product.

The participant does not ask for perfect data; she asks for enough context to justify the figures she presents. Inference: the underlying job is defending numbers in front of management, and the pain of having « no answer » for the director weighs more than the error itself.

> "If I know the data is from last night, I can explain it."
> - Claire Martin, [[interview-claire-martin]]

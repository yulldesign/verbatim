A single incident, where the director asked about a gap she could not explain, changed the participant's behaviour: she now cross-checks every number herself before trusting it. This is stated explicitly and tied to a concrete event; it explains why the reporting is redone outside the tool.

> "The director asked me where the gap came from and I had no answer. Since then, I don't trust the numbers until I've cross-checked them myself."
> - Claire Martin, [[interview-claire-martin]]
